Reading this comparison

J-Lens is a transport, not a label maker. Token lists come from the committed Iteration 1 output.

What changesThe learned transport estimates how this residual tends to influence later residuals.
What stays controlledActivation, layer, position, final norm, and vocabulary readout remain matched.
What it does not proveA readable token is descriptive evidence, not proof that the concept caused the answer.

Which token is predicted?

At position −1, the model predicts the first unseen token. Position −2 predicts a token already supplied by the prompt and can pass parity while answering the wrong question.
